# Public home page design

## Layout Structure
Apple.com is the reference for product-first hierarchy and paired actions. Ledger uses its own HTML product previews and branding. The proposed frame is a full-width landing page with a 1120px navigation container, centered hero and 1060px product stage. Feature sections use two equal columns above 660px and stack below it.

## Section Order
Navigation, short introduction strip, centered hero/dashboard preview, dark invoice section, inventory/proposal pair, actual available subscription plans, final login/registration invitation, footer.

## Navigation
A sticky 58px translucent header contains Ledger branding, feature anchors and Customer login. Mobile keeps branding and login while feature content remains reachable by normal scrolling. Customer login uses the existing company authentication route; no admin credentials are exposed.

## Typography
System sans-serif with 44-76px hero type, tight display tracking, 36-59px section headings and 16-23px supporting copy. Operational preview labels are smaller because they illustrate the interface rather than provide user input. Accessible page headings and descriptive preview labels remain available to assistive technology.

## Color System
Near-black #1d1d1f on white and #f5f5f7, blue #0071e3 for primary actions, a restrained blue/violet headline gradient. The invoice section uses a near-black background with white display text. Inventory and proposals use pale green and lavender surfaces. The same blue action color and neutral surfaces now extend across customer and admin workspaces.

## Spacing and Layout Rhythm
Hero top padding 67px on desktop/44px mobile. Feature sections use 58-80px vertical padding and 12px white gutters. Product illustrations sit 35-52px below primary actions; ordinary paragraphs have narrow readable measures.

## Image Treatment
All artwork is original, code-native HTML/CSS and local SVG images. Illustrated access panels use workspace-art.svg; proposal screens use proposal-art.svg. A bordered app window, invoice sheet, stock visualization and proposal card provide product imagery without Apple logos, photography or third-party assets. Sample figures are labelled illustrative and never read private customer records.

## Cards and Content Blocks
The hero is unboxed. Large promotional sections create the primary rhythm. Small rounded cards appear inside product demonstrations; actual plan cards have 22px radii and light borders. No fabricated testimonials or performance claims.

## Buttons and CTAs
Blue pill-shaped Customer login buttons are primary. Register your company is outlined when onboarding is open. Product exploration uses anchors and login links. Plan choices link to registration with a preselected plan. Focus rings, a skip link and reduced-motion support are included.

## Overall Design Feel
A calm, spacious product showcase inspired by Apple's current website hierarchy. The result is adapted to an Indian billing application and leads to real existing login, registration and company workflows.

The complete application uses premium.css: 20px cards, 24px illustrated page headers, 36px workspace headings, blue pill actions and focus rings. Login and registration pair an original illustrated panel with accessible existing forms. At 850px the access layout stacks; at 760px workspace navigation becomes a horizontally scrollable row. Reduced-motion and invoice print styles are preserved.
