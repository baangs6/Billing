Billing and Invoices refer to the same records; either selects invoices, their items and saved submission links. Service reminders are part of invoices.

Inventory resets retained product stock to zero and clears stock movements. Products may require Inventory, Invoices and Proposals. Customers may require Invoices and Proposals. Invoices may require Payments, Inventory and converted Proposals. The server checks existing links and blocks incomplete selections.

Finance clears manual income, expenses, opening balances and receipts. Invoice collections belong to Payments. Reports have no separate stored records: select their source sections to clear the corresponding reports. Invoice numbering and settings are kept.
